I notice my fists have clenched, and an unpleasant sensation is gathering in my stomach. The sensation then rises to my throat and spreads outward, cloudlike, filling my torso. Not content to rest there, the clouds soon invade and envelop my thoughts, as if to ridicule any so-called body-mind barrier.

What is causing me to feel this way? Did something horrible just happen? Well, yes and no. Something horrible didn't just happen and then cease to happen, with a clear beginning and end. Rather, I feel something horrible *is* happening and will continue to happen, and a horrible thing just reminded me of this.

The culprit in this case was a short video about the explosion of AI-generated music and its impact on small independent musicians like myself. 



This isn't an attempt to be objective about the impact of Generative AI on society and the planet.
